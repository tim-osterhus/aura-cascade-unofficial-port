# Aura Cascade Reimagined 0.2.1 NeoForge Beta

Approved beta for Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.252, and Java 21. Supports
client and dedicated-server environments. Requires Patchouli
1.21.1-93-NEOFORGE; Fabric API and Team Reborn Energy are not required. Back up
existing worlds before upgrading. Fabric-to-NeoForge world migration is not
validated; use a separate world for this beta.

This is the initial NeoForge loader beta on the existing 0.2.1 gameplay
baseline. It does not include the canonical 0.2.2 repairs and is not a claim of
full feature parity or exhaustive gameplay validation.

The native loader integration includes registries, networking, owner-private
accessory state, rendering, menus, event hooks, and NeoForge energy receivers.
It retains the `aura` mod/registry IDs and the amethyst-plus-eight-gold-nuggets
Aura Crystal recipe. QA helpers and the external Energy Meter test mod are
not bundled or required by the release.

The validation cycle found a fairy duplication risk during dimension/chunk
transitions. The candidate defers spawning until the owner's chunk ticks
entities, keeps orbit movement within ticking chunks, and rejects superseded
entities. The revised candidate passes the complete bounded multiplayer run,
including the same dimension transition that exposed the earlier duplication.

## Known Inherited Risks

The Fabric 0.2.1 baseline has these known issues:

- AC-022-001: Breeder accepts juveniles.
- AC-022-002: Duplicate offspring observed; causal explanation still under
  investigation.
- AC-022-003: Binding Ring costs link opens wrong entry.

These are disclosed as inherited risks only. They have not been confirmed as
NeoForge defects, and this beta does not claim to repair them.

## Coverage Limits

Fixtures supply raw materials and empty machines; their successful gameplay
outcomes are not injected. This is bounded acceptance, not a complete Survival
playthrough. Native powered bookshelf retrieval, every recipe/fairy role,
Angelsteel-specific animation, all guide hyperlinks, arbitrary chunk-unload
sequences, and broad modpack compatibility are not independently certified by
this initial loader gate. HUD review uses sampled captures across updates,
not pixel inspection of every rendered frame. Canonical 0.2.2 repairs will be
integrated and revalidated separately.
