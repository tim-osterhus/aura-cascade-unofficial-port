## Acceptance

The sixteenth final build passed 244 tests in 60 suites. All finite B1-B5
gameplay gates are supported by their bounded evidence, including independent
client review. Final intermediary packaged client run `20260923-121601-864`
passed world rendering and actual-font layout for all 41 entries, 371 pages
and 14 quests. Final multiplayer run `20260923-121724-053` passed all 15 state,
identity and lifecycle checks; all four JVM lifetimes exited 0. The actual
transformed-item lifetime, persistence and merge probe passed all three checks.
See [final validation](../audits/2026-09-23-sixteenth-release-validation.md)
and [the acceptance ledger](../audits/2026-09-22-video-acceptance.md).

Screenshots were reviewed, but multiplayer fairy identity/synchronization rests
on server/client UUID and state evidence, not an unobstructed fairy rendering
in every camera view. Supplied-material fixtures do not establish a completely
natural survival acquisition chain. All-role permutations, external energy
receivers, third-party modpacks, real-time full Red Hole lifetime expiry and
large-server endurance remain disclosed beta coverage limits.

The final build, development client, packaged client and multiplayer workload
had peak working set/private allocation in MiB of 1391.6/1501.1,
1343.1/1898.3, 1252.8/1782.1 and 2510.7/3025.1 respectively. Heavy workloads
were serialized. These samples exclude desktop/agent processes as requested;
one-second or 250-ms sampling is not a hard operating-system memory cap.
