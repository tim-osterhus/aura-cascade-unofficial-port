## Acceptance And Limits

The [release gate](../audits/2026-09-28-neoforge-021-release-gate.md) and
[independent review](../audits/2026-09-28-neoforge-021-independent-review.md)
record final-artifact acceptance: 265 regression tests, 18 publisher tests,
six packaged UI runs, 31 multiplayer state/action checks, five native hooks,
eight energy cases including an external Energy Meter, White Arcane Ingot
progression, and a normal-stop/distinct-process disk restart. The last
regression rerun also passed, and did not change the release JAR.

All owned Minecraft processes exited normally and all reserved slots were
returned to Mod Playtesting before publishing. The coordinated limit was four
Minecraft JVMs globally, with at most three clients and one dedicated server.

This is an initial loader beta, not exhaustive feature certification. Known
inherited Breeder and guide-link risks, untested powered bookshelf retrieval,
Angelsteel-specific animation, broader recipes/fairy roles/modpacks, and
unverified Fabric-world migration are disclosed in the
[beta notes](0.2.1-neoforge-beta.md). No canonical 0.2.2 repairs were imported;
that integration awaits the user's later handoff.
