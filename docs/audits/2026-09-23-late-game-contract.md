# Specialized late-game contract (2026-09-23)

## Version-matched evidence

Source is the local `AuraCascade-592.jar`, inspected with `javap -c -p` for `LootTile`, `SpawnTile`, `MinerTile`, `EntityMinerExplosion`, `TileRitualNether`, `TileRitualEnd`, and `OreDropManager`. All five tile variants report `validItemsNearby() == true`; `LateGameBlockEntity.hasValidWork()` exposes that Monitor contract without predicting whether a particular world action will succeed. The max-progress and power-per-progress pairs remain looter `100/5000`, spawner `15/190`, miner `1/2500`, and both rituals `100/5000`.

| Variant | 592 behavior | Bounded 1.21.1 behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Looter | `ChestGenHooks.getOneItem("dungeonChest", random)` retries IMC-blacklisted items, drops one stationary item at block `y + 1.5`. | Rolls the built-in `SIMPLE_DUNGEON` chest table, chooses one nonempty generated stack, and drops it at the original position with zero velocity and normal despawn. The old fixed eight-entry item/count table is removed. A full-table roll followed by one selection is not the exact old weighted-entry operation, and the removed Forge IMC blacklist has no replacement here. |
| Spawner | Selects a natural `MONSTER` spawn-list entry from the world at the tile position, creates one mob, and positions it at `y + 2`. | Uses the chunk generator's weighted `getMobsAt` result for the current biome/structure and creates one mob at the same position. The invented dimension pools, six-mob cap, and two-air-block gate are removed. Empty spawn lists leave work blocked. |
| Miner | The pulse latches until the next power-use cycle. A moving charged entity gains one charge, expires 100 ticks after its last charge, stresses every fortified block in a 5-cube around its collision, and explodes at strength 50 if none exists. A pulse disarms it and drops `floor(charge^1.5 / 50)` ore-dictionary results only above charge 20. | A saved 1x1 helper entity starts at miner `y - 1.5`, moves by its random bounce velocity, checks collisions, stresses every fortified block in its 5-cube, and creates a flaming strength-50 explosion if uncontained. Its charge, last-charge time, last-explosion time, and source miner are saved. The miner resolves its UUID, adds charge, or disarms it on a latched pulse. The 100-tick expiry, logarithmic explosion cooldown, charge threshold, and ore-yield formula follow 592. Vanilla ore weights remain; present `c:ores` item/block tags add modern or modded ore items with a neutral weight of 1,000 each. |
| Nether/End rituals | On power use, capture the source biome. Each tick processes up to 31 queued source-biome columns within squared radius 22,500, rewrites mapped blocks from world bottom to top, sets target biome, expands in four horizontal directions, then removes the ritual block. No direct entity damage, levitation, blaze, or endermite burst is in these tile methods. | A persisted queue processes two 4x4 biome cells (32 columns) per tick, converting blocks only inside the 150-block circle, rewriting biome palettes across world height, marking chunks dirty, and resending biome data. It self-removes when the queue ends. The modern 4x4 biome granularity can recolor edge cells up to three blocks past the block-conversion boundary. The invented random 24-position conversion and direct danger effects are removed. |

The Nether mapping is stone to netherrack; grass/dirt to one-third soul sand or netherrack; old logs/leaves to glowstone; short grass to nether wart; gravel/sand to soul sand; water to lava; ice/snow layer to air. The End mapping is stone/grass/dirt/gravel/sand to end stone; old logs/leaves to obsidian; short grass/ice/snow layer to air. The 592 End tile changes water only when the optional Forge `ender` fluid exists, so this dependency-free port leaves water unchanged. Modern acacia and dark-oak logs/leaves are included because they were the second legacy log/leaf block variants; newer wood families are not silently mapped.

The 592 miner entity has no custom renderer or entity texture. Its client visual is an explosion particle every other tick; the existing miner block texture remains the original asset. This port registers an invisible `NoopRenderer` and emits that vanilla particle, without inventing a replacement texture. Original Forge ore-dictionary enumeration and cross-mod generation weights cannot be reconstructed from modern registries: `c:ores` tags expose only the actual installed entries, and the 1,000 weight for a newly tagged entry is an explicit compatibility policy, not an original numeric weight. The helper entity requires common registration through `MinerExplosionEntities.bootstrapCommon()` and client renderer registration through `MinerExplosionClient.bootstrapClient()` before world use.

## Persistence, inspection, and validation

Stored power, progress, miner UUID/charge/pulse/timing, last power/result, source biome, and ritual queue are persisted. Older fixed-charge miner saves without an entity UUID spawn a moving entity with their saved charge and timing. The immutable `inspectionSnapshot()` contains progress, maximum, stored/last power, miner charge, last result, and queued ritual-cell count for parent HUD wiring. Chunk biome mutations are marked unsaved and sent to clients; regular state uses block-entity update packets. Focused tests cover conversion tables, cell/radius boundaries, natural-list selection, dungeon-roll selection, miner formulas and bounce scale, and tagged ore eligibility. No Gradle, build, or client launch was run in this agent lane. Integration must still verify entity save/reload and delayed chunk loading, client particles, collision/containment/explosion, ore tag reloads, biome persistence, HUD updates, spawn behavior on Peaceful and normal difficulty, and the looter's table distribution.
