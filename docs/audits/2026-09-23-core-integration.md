## Inspection and Block Behavior

The client now renders consumer progress/max, base power cost, Last Power and
current stored power separately. Vortex readouts identify the output, individual
items, colored requirements and receipts. White requirements display "Any color"
because the original recipe semantics accept any falling-power color.

The pedestal selection/collision cube is 0.25-0.75 in each axis and does not
occlude adjacent faces, matching both its shipped model and the original
AuraBlock constructor. The controller keeps its full-cube model.

Consumers, controller and pedestals expose comparator readings without emitting
direct neighbor redstone. Original AuraBlock/ConsumerBlock have comparator
overrides but no direct-power override. Direct emission in the port could
inhibit a neighboring pump as soon as processing began.
